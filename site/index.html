Haliable

The halal food scanner that shows its work.

Scan any grocery barcode and get a halal verdict in seconds — checked ingredient by ingredient against the standard you follow, from strict zabiha to general halal.

Honest by design

  • Worst-case first. If an ingredient's origin can't be verified, we say so — we never guess "halal".
  • Real citations. Every ingredient grade is backed by published rulings from certifiers and fatwa bodies you can read yourself.
  • Your standard. Zabiha-only, Hanafi-strict, general, or lenient — verdicts adapt to the position you choose.
  • Certifier aware. Recognizes marks like HMC, HFSAA, IFANCA, JAKIM and MUI, and knows what each one actually guarantees.
  • Find halal near you. A community map of halal stores and restaurants, confirmed by people who've been there.

Private by default

Scanning needs no account. Lookups send only the barcode number — never your identity. Read the privacy policy.
