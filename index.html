Islamic Standards

What makes food Halal?

We built the most comprehensive, research-backed guide to Islamic dietary law in the context of modern American food production.

🚫
No Pork or Derivatives

Pork, lard, gelatin from pigs, and any porcine-derived additives are strictly prohibited — including processing aids that may not appear on standard labels.

🔪
Zabiha Slaughter

Meat must be slaughtered according to Zabiha — a swift cut, bismillah recitation, and complete blood drainage. Stunning methods vary by scholarly opinion.

🍷
Alcohol-Free

All intoxicants are haram, including alcohol used as a flavor carrier, preservative, or processing aid — a common hidden issue in packaged foods.

🧬
Clean Additives

Emulsifiers, enzymes, colorants, and other additives must have halal-compliant sourcing. E-numbers and coded ingredients are where most surprises hide.
