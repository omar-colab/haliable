## Description (≤4000 chars)
```
Haliable is the halal food scanner that respects both your deen and your privacy.

Point your camera at any packaged product's barcode and Haliable reads the
ingredients and gives you a clear verdict — Halal, Questionable, or Haram —
backed by the published positions of recognized halal certification bodies.

WHY HALIABLE

• Source-cited, not guesswork. Every verdict reports what real standards bodies
  and scholarly references say. Haliable reports positions; it does not issue
  independent religious rulings. When a source can't be confirmed, we tell you
  it's "questionable" instead of pretending.

• Built for caution. A false "halal" is the worst error, so anything we can't
  positively confirm — including products with no ingredient data — is shown as
  questionable, never assumed halal.

• Truly private. Scanning runs entirely on your device. Your scan history stays
  on your phone. No ads, no tracking, no analytics SDKs.

• Choose your standard. From Zabiha-only to general — set how strict you want
  Haliable to be, and verdicts adjust.

• Find halal near you. A community map of halal groceries, butchers, bakeries,
  and restaurants.

• Stuck on a tricky label? Send a photo for a human review.

Browsing and scanning never require an account. Sign in with Apple only if you
want to contribute a review or add a store.

Product data from Open Food Facts (ODbL) and USDA FoodData Central.

Always verify with a scholar you trust for your situation.
```
