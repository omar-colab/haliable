# Proposed new citation subjects: additives with no rule yet (Oct 23, 2026)

**Status: PENDING OWNER APPROVAL. Nothing here is in the app.** Report, don't rule: these
additives grade *Questionable* today only because no cited source covers them.

Why it matters: in a 314-product US probe, 195 products graded Questionable, mostly because of
these codes ("In probe" = products in the probe containing it).

**20 codes are listed Halal with no condition** by Source A; the rest are conditional.

## What approving means

1. Each approved code gets a seeded GradeMatrix row for all four standards: draft Halal for
   "no condition" codes; conditional codes stay Questionable (widely accepted) until origin is known.
2. Candidates go through the normal pipeline (docs/citations/candidates/, then CitationCommitter).
   Section 8: lenient and high-traffic cells need two independent sources. Source B covers only E101, E102,
   E110 and E440, so most codes need a second source before they can clear.
3. Disagreements to rule on: E101 riboflavin (A: Mushbooh unless plant; B: Halaal, microbial/synthetic)
   and E440 pectin (A: Halal; B: Halaal, but check it is not from cider making).

## Source C (tier 1, government): Indonesia KMA 1360/2021

- **Keputusan Menteri Agama No. 1360 Tahun 2021 tentang Bahan yang Dikecualikan dari Kewajiban Bersertifikat Halal**
  (Minister of Religious Affairs decree: materials exempt from mandatory halal certification), issued under
  Law 33/2014 and PP 39/2021, published by BPJPH:
  <https://cmsbl.halal.go.id/uploads/KMA_1360_tahun_2021_tentang_Bahan_yang_dikecualikan_dari_kewajiban_bersertifikat_Halal_5a41b7b4e1.pdf>
- Entries below are in Annex section "c. Bahan Kimia yang Tidak Tergolong Berbahaya dan Tidak Mengandung…",
  list "2. Bahan Kimia Hasil Sintesis Anorganik dan Organik" (synthetic chemicals), except E414/E406 (list 1, plant
  materials without processing). The decree's basis: the material is "dikategorikan tidak berisiko mengandung Bahan
  yang diharamkan" (categorised as not at risk of containing haram material).
- Caveat: an exemption from certification is a risk classification, not a fatwa on every product. It is still the
  halal authority's own published position.

Disagreements to rule on: **E306 tocopherols** (A: haram if from pork fat; C: exempt), **E341 / E170**
(A: conditional on a mineral source; C: exempt), **E101 riboflavin** (A: Mushbooh; B: Halaal).

Still missing a second source: E330 citric acid (64 products), E415, E300, E331, E412, E420, E202, E270, E101.

**Note on citric acid & friends:** E330, E300, E415, E270, E420 and E101 are *absent* from KMA 1360's exempt list,
whose only fermentation category is unprocessed traditional foods (tape, oncom, tempe). Indonesia therefore treats
these fermentation-made additives as **critical** (the growth medium and processing aids must be checked).
That argues for keeping them Questionable (widely accepted, grade 2), not Halal, unless a second Halal source appears.

## Ready-to-approve package (branch `proposal/additive-citations`)

The 11 two-source additives are staged as 44 candidates (`docs/citations/candidates/cell-E{200,250,301,339,340,406,407,414,440,460,461}-*.json`,
`"decision": "staged"`). Each is keyed on its single known origin (synthetic or plant), because §8.4 forbids clearing an
*unspecified*-origin cell to Halal. E260/E262 were dropped: acetic acid can come from (wine) vinegar, so it has no single origin.
A dry run through `CitationCommitter` shows **44/44 would clear** once approved.

To approve:
1. Flip `"staged"` to `"approved"` in the files you accept (or `"rejected"` / `"needsBetterSource"`).
2. Write Citations.json through the gate, never by hand:
   `TEST_RUNNER_HALIABLE_COMMIT=1 TEST_RUNNER_HALIABLE_CANDIDATES_DIR=$PWD/docs/citations/candidates TEST_RUNNER_HALIABLE_CITATIONS_JSON=$PWD/Haliable/Resources/Citations.json xcodebuild test … -only-testing:HaliableTests/CitationPipelineTests`
3. Regenerate the ledger (`TEST_RUNNER_HALIABLE_WRITE_CITATIONS=1 …`), run the full suite, then merge the branch.

Until then, this branch shows these 11 additives as **draft Halal (pending review)**. Don't merge it unapproved.
